# HunterGearAdvisor — design

Addon for WoW Forever (client `wowf` 1.60.1.x, interface 16001) that tells the user's BM hunter whether an item is an upgrade.
Stat knowledge lives in project memory `hunter-stat-weights.md` (ZygorCustomGuides project). Research notes are in `docs/research/`.
The two modes by the character's level (below 60 levelling, at 60 the raid boss) and the mechanics HGA takes from
wowsims/forever in both are designed in `docs/superpowers/specs/raid-mode-design.md` (below "RMD") and
`docs/superpowers/specs/raid-mode-w1-open-design.md` (W1's open questions the wowsims way); this document keeps the
addon's overall design and points there for the detail.

## What the user asked for (their words, 2026-11-12)

- Green checkmark on the item icon in the inventory if equipping it gives a gain.
- In the item tooltip, a number for the gain ("цифра усиления").
- For melee weapons, the tooltip says where to equip it (main hand / off hand).
- If a two-hander is equipped, analyse which two one-handers to wear, so the user knows whether to swap the 2H for two 1H.
- In quest rewards, see which offered item gives a gain.
- Items above the player's level keep the checkmark (user: "я сам пойму что она выше уровня").
- The hunter avoids melee; everything that matters is ranged damage. Refined 2026-11-17 («мили атака вообще ни в учет
  хант ее избегает кроме сурв ветки»): melee counts for no hunter but the Survival branch (strictly the most talent points
  in Survival), in both modes, and that branch's melee is wowsims' SV Weave (RMD 3, 15).
- Health, armor and mana come after damage: with equal damage the item with more survival or mana
  is better, with a balance between the two (400 mana clearly beats 100 HP); "damage always has
  priority" — any damage difference decides. By the mode (2026-11-17): so while levelling (below 60); at 60 the mana is
  part of the damage as in wowsims (the user's answer 4 = (b): the mana limits the casts, and Intellect, MP5, Spirit and
  mana get a damage value, RMD 7), and after damage only the survival is compared, its armor against the level-63 boss's
  hits (RMD 7.9).
- The talents the character actually has must be taken into account (including the one that
  turns Intellect into attack power, Careful Aim).

## What the user decided later (their words, 2026-11-17)

- Two modes by the level: «если не 60 то на прокачку, если 60 значит на рейд», «наша цель играть против босов в рейда».
  Below 60 the target is a mob of the hunter's own level (5% hit cap); at 60 it is wowsims' hunter preset's raid boss:
  level 63, +3 (8% hit cap; answer 6 = (a)), armor 3731 less one major (Sunder Armor or Expose Armor, 2250) and one minor
  (Faerie Fire or Curse of Recklessness, 505) reduction, 976, less the hunter's own armor penetration (answer 3 = (b), O5),
  a dragonkin (answer 5 = (a): +AP against beasts and Beastslaying count 0), with Curse of the Elements, Gift of Arthas
  and Judgement of Wisdom on it (O7 = (a)) and the pet behind it (RMD 1, 2, 5). «просто блять на прокачке на мой уровень
  на 60 на рейды»: no setting picks the mode or the target; the hit cap follows the target (RMD 14.2).
- «берем как в вов симс»: the mechanics as wowsims has them, in both modes (RMD 4): the level difference capped at +3,
  crit suppression against a higher-level target, the white swings as one roll, the pet's and the hawks' hit, Beastslaying
  as flat damage on every weapon strike and shot, attack power against a creature type on the hunter's own attacks, the
  procs by their DB2 trigger and DefenseType, the two Parse gaps (the ranged-only hit line, Counterattack Lodestone); W1's
  other open questions likewise (O3, W1D).
- Buffs: the live character sheet as it is (answer 2 = (a)), and its stat multipliers count (O8 = (a): an item's
  Agility, Strength, Intellect and Spirit under Greater Blessing of Kings and the like, RMD 4.8, 21).
- The raid fight as wowsims' preset runs it: 180 ± 5 s, the major cooldowns from 5 s aligned, Serpent Sting re-cast when
  it falls, the pet alive all fight, Aimed Shot in the shared cooldown (the agreed defaults, RMD 6); the mana as wowsims
  (answer 4 = (b), RMD 7); the preset's in-fight consumables always on (O2 = (a): the Major Frenzy Potion at 5 s, the
  Major Mana Potion at 125 s if it fits, else a second Frenzy Potion, the Demonic Rune by its fit; RMD 7.4); a Use buff's
  attack power never reaches the pet (O6, both modes).
- «нахуй ты спамишь командами… у адона 2 функции: показать что одеть и вкладка с бис шмоткой», «убирай нахуй»: no tuning
  command and no setting. `/hga target`, `/hga melee`, `/hga weave`, `/hga cap`, `/hga ppm`, `/hga min` and `/hga banner`
  went with their saved settings; `/hga weights`, `/hga dump` (read-only diagnostics), `/hga swap` (the list of what to
  put on) and `/hga preraid` (`/hga pr`, the BiS window) stay; any damage gain gets the checkmark; the swap banner is
  always on, its × hides it until the plan changes (RMD 14).
- The Survival branch's melee and weave as wowsims' SV Weave preset (RMD 15; its shared-category shot Multi-Shot, U3); the
  unarmed main hand at 1.0 s as wowsims (RMD 16); DoT procs priced as wowsims prices them (U2 = (a), RMD 17, 22); no proc
  rate made up (U4 = (a), RMD 18.6).

## Decisions made by me (not asked)

- Bags: Baganator corner widget (user runs Baganator). Fallback: hook Blizzard `ContainerFrameMixin:UpdateItems` per container frame when Baganator is not loaded.
- Quest rewards: hook `QuestInfo_Display` (Forever loads the Mainline QuestInfo; the quest log is the map QuestInfo with `QuestInfoFrame.questLog`). Mark every upgrade reward, highlight the best choice.
- Tooltip: `TooltipDataProcessor.AddTooltipPostCall(Enum.TooltipDataType.Item, ...)` on GameTooltip and ItemRefTooltip only.
- Gain unit: the weights' unit, plus approximate DPS (gain × the DPS of one unit from the model). The unit is 1 RAP whenever more RAP adds damage; where it adds none (no bow, gun or crossbow worn; no hawks; no pet, or a pet whose attack power is the melee one) it is 1 melee attack power; where neither adds any (a hunter below level 10, or with Lone Wolf and no pet out, with no bow, gun or crossbow: nothing attacks) it is 1 DPS. `/hga weights` names the unit.
- Weights are computed live by the damage model, `Core/Model.lua` (`Model.Parts` / `Model.Total` give the damage per second of the hunter, the pet, the dive and the hawks; `Model.Weights` takes the derivatives in the weights' unit), from its inputs (the `p` fields of `Model.Parts`): level (`level`), ranged and melee attack power (`rap`, `map`), ranged and melee crit (`crit`, `meleeCrit`), the hit of every attack (`hit`), the weapon skill of the ranged hand and of each melee hand over 5 x level (`rangedSkill`, `mhSkill`, `ohSkill`) and the hit the ranged and the main hand's skill give (`rangedHit`, `meleeHit`), expertise (`expertise`), the five attack speed inputs by source (`haste`, `attackSpeed`, `hasteRating`, `meleeHaste`, `rangedHaste`), the ranged weapon, ammo and quiver (`bowDPS`, `bowSpeed`, `ammoDPS`, `quiver`), the melee weapons (`mhDPS`, `mhSpeed`, `mhNorm`, `ohDPS`, `ohSpeed`, `twoH`, `unarmed`), whether a bow, gun or crossbow is worn, no shots without one (`noShots`), the Survival branch, strictly the most talent points in Survival, which weaves as wowsims' SV Weave preset (`survival`; every other build has no melee), the mode (`mode`: `"level"` below 60, `"raid"` at 60, `Model.RAID_LEVEL`) and the target's level difference by it (`mobDiff`: 0 below 60, 3 at 60), the sheet's Agility (`agi`), the worn gear's ranged-only hit (`rangedGearHit`), Beastslaying (`beastSlaying`), attack power against the target's creature type (`apVsTarget`), spell hit and spell crit (`spellHit`, `spellCrit`), the live sheet's stat multipliers (`statMult`), in raid mode the mana pool, the regens and Spirit (`manaMax`, `regenRest`, `regenCast`, `spirit`) and armor penetration (`armorPen`), the set's Use buffs in raid mode (`uses`, optional), the Orc racial Command, +5% pet damage (`orc`), the pet's state, family and swing time (`petOut`, `petFamily`, `petSwing`), the Serpent Sting rank, IX from the book when it is known (`serpentTick`), and the talent ranks (`tal`); so they follow the character while leveling. Levelling runs one pass with the cooldowns at their averages; raid mode runs the 180 s fight against the raid boss (RMD 6, 7: time segments with Rapid Fire's, Bestial Wrath's, the Use buffs' and the consumables' windows, the cast tempo, the mana's regimes) and takes each weight as the fight's derivative. `tools/weights_model.py` is an outdated level-43 prototype of the model (a fixed armor multiplier 0.70, the pet's haste x1.02, glancing x0.95; no shots-off case, shots' own hit, pet swing time, attack speed inputs or armor by level) that the addon does not use. Talent ranks are read live from the trait tree (`C_ClassTalents.GetActiveConfigID`, `C_Traits.GetNodeInfo(...).activeRank`, node ids from DB2 tree 1091), as applied: clicks staged before "Apply" are left out while an applied read of that config exists. There is no fallback build: while the client has no talent data yet, or a config has staged changes and no applied read, no evaluation is shown and the tooltip and `/hga` (`weights`, `dump`, `swap`) say why, while the swap banner stays hidden (`Model.DEFAULT_TALENTS`, the 2026-11-12 screenshot build, serves the tests and the reference numbers only).
- Secondary comparison (my call on the formula): survival and mana as % of the character's current health and mana pools; armor as effective health via the Classic armor formula; MP5 = 12 mana; Spirit = 1 + 6 × min(1, s) mana (per minute of combat, `Model.SecondaryRates`), s the share of Spirit regen that keeps going while casting: 0.25 × Bestial Discipline rank + 0.25 × Rapid Recuperation rank × 0.8 (Serpent Sting's uptime, `CONST.serpentUptime`) + 0.5 × 0.4 × Resourcefulness rank (Resourcefulness 440529: a melee or ranged ability crit triggers the 30 s buff 1242688 with a 50% chance a rank, and the buff keeps 50% of the regen going; 0.4 is its uptime per rank, `CONST.resourcefulnessUptime`, an estimate); with Bestial Discipline alone that is 1 + 1.5 × its rank. Raid mode (RMD 7.9): no mana part (the mana stats are damage there), the armor against the level-63 boss's hits.
- Checkmark threshold 0: any damage gain gets the checkmark (the user's rule); no setting changes it (`/hga min` went with the user's decision of 2026-11-17, RMD 14.3), and `Game/Init.lua` removes the saved settings table at load, whatever version saved it (RMD 14.6).
- Item stats come from tooltip lines (`C_TooltipInfo.GetHyperlink`) parsed with the client's own localized templates (`_G[...]`), so ruRU and enUS both work. Decimal comma handled via `DECIMAL_SEPERATOR`.
- Weights come from the worn set: the damage model runs at the worn state (the live sheet values, the worn weapons, the worn ranged skill's effect). With no bow, gun or crossbow worn (an empty ranged slot, a thrown weapon, Egan's Blaster) the model casts no shot, so every weight prices a hunter without shots — a bag bow's own stats and every other piece too. The swap plan's first step then puts the bag bow on (its weapons go through the model with the bow's shots); the next plan, with the bow worn, prices with shots.
- Hit is scored on the whole equipped set and capped at the cap of the target the mode gives (`Model.HitCap(mobDiff)`: 5% against a mob of the hunter's level below 60, 8% against the +3 raid boss at 60; no override, RMD 14.2). The base hit (the live hit less the worn items' and worn set bonuses' hit) is kept as it is, negative under a hit debuff, so the worn set's hit is the live hit; hit below 0% is priced linearly, as the model's miss chance grows. Expertise is scored on the whole set the same way: the items' and set bonuses' expertise (the model's own expertise, as no live value is read), a Use buff's on top, capped at the mob's dodge (`Model.MobAvoid`: 5% at an equal-level mob, 0.5% more a level above), where every use of it in the model clamps (the pet's dodge and parry, the hawks' and the melee swings' dodge), and priced at its weight below the cap, which the model takes at expertise 0. `/hga weights` prints the worn set's expertise and the cap.
- Unusable: red restriction lines in the tooltip (except the level requirement) or a proficiency the hunter lacks (maces, wands, shields, plate, thrown in the ranged slot, mail before it is trained). No checkmark, tooltip says why.
- "Chance on hit" damage procs, and "Equip:" lines that are chance-on-hit damage (Heartseeking Crossbow), linked to the Forever DB2 (`Game/ProcData.lua`, generated by `tools/gen_proc_data.py`: the trigger, its ProcTypeMask, chance, procs per minute and internal cooldown, the damage spell's mean, school, DefenseType and whether it can crit, its damage over time; RMD 4.6, 17; a proc links by the mean of the range its line reads, a damage of one number by the amount its line states, a damage over time alone by its item's one line Parse leaves unread: RMD 17.4, 24.2). The rate (U4 = (a), as wowsims, no rate made up; RMD 18.6): the item's rate measured in Forever logs (wowsims docs/evidence, `Score.MEASURED_PPM`), else wowsims' override row of its entry (`tools/database/overrides`, read as data into `Game/ProcData.lua` with its source: a Forever measurement or a rate carried over from Classic or TBC, unchecked in Forever), else the DB2's own procs per minute or chance 1-99 of an equip aura; a proc with none is not priced and its tooltip line says its rate is unknown (a re-issued twin of a measured weapon included: wowsims has no row for it). A rate is per minute at the speed of the weapon in the hand that hits (wowsims' PPM managers), a chance per hit, an internal cooldown as a dead time: `Score.ProcRate` prices it on the hunter's landed hits of the scored set's own model point (`Model.LandedHits` at the model point with that set's weapon inputs, the run its weapon gain takes, `ctx.landedHitsFor`: the Survival weave's white swings, Raptor Strike, Strider Kick, Mongoose Bite and Wing Clip and its shots, else the shots from range and the hawks' dives, the dive a ranged ability; RMD 4.6.3). A linked equip aura rolls on the hits its ProcTypeMask names (white melee, melee abilities, Auto Shot, ranged abilities, and Serpent Sting's landed applications where the harmful-spell bit clears RequireDamageDealt, never for the Highborne Research Tablet; RMD 4.6.2); a weapon's "Chance on hit" rolls on every landed hit of its hand, Serpent Sting's applications, which deal no damage, among a ranged weapon's (wowsims `sim/core/spelldata/resolve_proc.go:95-110` WeaponProc; EV-0021: Rend, Concussion Blow and Sunder Armor applications proc Dragon's Call); each hand's hits at the speed of the weapon in that hand in that set, so a weapon's own hand at its own speed. A proc's damage rolls the table of its damage spell's DefenseType at the main hand's entry (`Model.ProcOutcomes`: the spell miss and spell crit of the magic table, 17% against the +3 boss; the main hand's physical miss, dodge and suppressed crit of the melee and ranged tables; with no DefenseType by the school), with the hunter's damage multipliers, armor on a physical school, Curse of the Elements on a magic one and Gift of Arthas' +8 on a physical one in raid mode (RMD 4.6.4, 5.3); a damage over time (RMD 17, 22) deals its ticks between two landed applications, `Score.DotTicks`, at its table's application chance and its ticks' crit, no armor on a bleed; the tooltip shows the rate and the procs a minute in the set the item is scored in: "с твоими атаками сейчас" on the worn set's hits (a worn item, an item that goes into no set), "если надеть" on the hits of the set the evaluation places the item in (`res.newSet`): a bag item, an item of `where.kind` "new" (a quest reward, loot, a merchant's, mail, trade or auction item, another unit's worn item, `Game/Tooltip.lua`) or a chat link that is not worn (`A.ModelProcRates`' second value). Other effects listed as "not counted".
- "Use:" stat buffs: `Game/UseData.lua` (generated by `tools/gen_use_data.py` from the Forever DB2 item effects) prices each over its average uptime, duration / cooldown, without the effects that need a condition the spell states, and one buff per Use spell in a set (two copies of one item share its cooldown and its aura); Use lines that are no stat buff, or a buff that events use up (SpellAuraOptions ProcCharges), stay "not counted". That is levelling; raid mode runs a set's Use buffs through the fight in their windows from 5 s, a burst trinket after another's, with Rapid Fire and Bestial Wrath (`ctx.useGain`, RMD 6.3, 6.8), and the pet keeps the attack power of its summon (O6, both modes: a Use buff's attack power never reaches the pet).
- Weapon skill by wowsims' attack table (the user's rule for W1's open questions, 2026-11-17:
  `docs/superpowers/specs/raid-mode-w1-open-design.md` section 3; wowsims `sim/core/target.go:549-615`): each hand rolls
  its own entry by the skill of the weapon it holds, its bonus over the level's maximum 5 x level (0 for an empty hand).
  The miss is 5% + 0.1% a point of the gap 5 x the level difference - bonus while the gap is 10 points or less, else 5%
  + 0.2% a point (`Model.SkillMiss`; 8% at the level's skill against a raid boss, the client's
  `CR_HUNTER_HIT_CAP_TOOLTIP`); dodge 0.1% a point; glancing chance and damage and the crit suppression (0.2% a point
  below the maximum) from the skill below the maximum, skill above it lowering the miss and dodge and raising the glance
  damage only. The ranged hand (a bow, gun or crossbow) gives the shots their own hit (`Model.SkillHit`, priced through
  the shots' cap at W.HIT_RANGED) and its suppression on the shots, Serpent Sting's ticks and the dive (W.SKILL_CRIT a
  point below the maximum, W.SKILL_CRIT_NO_SHOTS for a set without shots, which has bonus 0); a set's skill counts its
  own ranged line's item and set-bonus points, and a ranged weapon of another type fights at its own line's trained
  skill (the tooltip shows the hit and crit change). The melee hands (the ten melee skill lines, parsed from the tooltip
  and the set bonuses) count in the Survival branch's weave: each set's weapons run through the model at that set's
  hand skills (`Score.MeleeSkills`, the weapon run of `ctx.weaponGain`), the main hand's skill hit through the melee cap
  at W.HIT_MELEE, the dual-wield part per hand in its own room past the cap; a proc's melee and ranged tables are the main
  hand's entry in every build. A set without a bow, gun or crossbow (an empty ranged slot, Egan's Blaster, a thrown weapon)
  casts no shot; a worn thrown weapon keeps the level's skill.
- Pet and hawk crit (wowsims `sim/hunter/pets.go:142-153` inheritedCrit; raid-mode W1 design section 4): the higher of
  the owner's melee sheet crit and ranged sheet crit, each less 0.04% a point of its own hand's skill below the level's
  maximum (nothing above it), at least 0; the hawks take half of it (`summon_hawk.go:83`), Ferocity whole on both; all of
  it is aura crit on their own tables (level 60, skill 300), suppressed at higher-level targets.
- The hawks' dive (raid-mode W1 design section 5): its rank's base damage with Unleashed Fury's 3 and Ferocity's 2 a rank
  added as flat damage (each talent's SPELLMOD_EFFECT1 row on the dive's class mask, wowsims
  `sim/hunter/talents_beast_mastery.go:57-84`), before crit, armor and the multipliers, at every rank.
- Enchant lines: the "+2% Crit" scope counts as ranged crit (W.CRIT_RANGED: the enchant's spell needs a bow, gun or crossbow, DB2 SpellEquippedItems); Beastslaying as wowsims has it (RMD 4.5): flat damage added to each physical weapon strike's and shot's base damage before the attacker's multipliers, armor and the outcome (white swings, Auto Shot, Aimed, Multi, Sniper Shot, Raptor, Mongoose, Strider Kick and Wing Clip; not Arcane Shot, the dive, Serpent Sting's ticks, the pet or the hawks), the set's main hand's and off hand's summed, at the mode's share of beasts (0.3 while levelling, 0 against the dragonkin boss); the attack speed enchants by the scope of their aura (DB2 SpellItemEnchantment and SpellEffect): Minor Haste on gloves (aura 342) as attack speed, Iron Counterweight on two-handers and Automatic Crowd Pummeler on shoulders (aura 319) as melee-only attack speed, Arcanum of Rapidity on head and legs (auras 319 + 140) as melee-only and ranged-only attack speed, each at its own weight (a weapon's like its other stats).
- Weapons are priced through the damage model at their own speed; a weapon without a parsed speed or DPS is an error. Dual-wield hit past the cap counts only beyond the worn hit, which the model already prices. Without a bow, gun or crossbow in the ranged slot no shot is cast (DB2 SpellEquippedItems of every shot), so taking the bow off loses every shot; Summon Hawk and the pet stay (the Survival branch's weave needs its shots: without a bow, gun or crossbow it neither shoots nor melees, RMD 15), and the dive then takes no Ranged Weapon Specialization, which needs such a weapon too (its SpellEquippedItems row). An empty main hand is wowsims' unarmed weapon, 1.0 s and a normalized 1.0 (RMD 16).
- Item set bonuses: `Game/SetData.lua` (generated by `tools/gen_set_data.py` from the Forever DB2: ItemSet for the sets, names and profession rank, ItemSetSpell for every bonus, SpellEffect for what each bonus spell does) gives each set's bonuses as stats; a set counts its distinct worn item ids, its hit joins the items' hit under the cap, the worn set's haste and expertise join the worn items' own in the damage model's inputs, a profession set counts only at the ItemSet rank (skill rank + modifier), a crit bound to ranged weapons alone (Dread Hunter's Chain's 3 pieces: crit with bows, guns, thrown weapons and crossbows) counts as ranged crit (W.CRIT_RANGED, like the crit scope), and bonuses that are not plain stats are listed as "not counted". The swap plan also tries putting on two or more bag pieces of one set together.
- Attack speed: the model has five attack speed inputs, the worn state's permanent attack speed by source, each the
  product of its sources (`Player.lua` `wornHaste`), who takes each by wowsims' paths (the user's rule for W1's open
  questions; raid-mode W1 design section 7): `haste`, an item's or enchant's aura 342 (Minor Haste on gloves; wowsims
  registers it as melee and ranged speed, `sim/core/enchant_speed.go`), the shots, the melee swings, the pet and the hawks;
  `attackSpeed`, a set bonus's aura 9 or 342 (the Tier-1 two-piece bonuses, `ATTACK_SPEED` in `Game/SetData.lua`) and the
  Skyborne racial Wind Blessed (wowsims' AttackSpeedMultiplier, MultiplyAttackSpeed), the shots, the melee swings and the
  pet, not the hawks; `hasteRating`, haste rating (the tooltip's "attack speed and casting speed by N%" line and the
  rating line, `HASTE_RATING`; wowsims' hasteRatingMultiplier on the owner's totals), the shots and the melee swings,
  neither the pet nor the hawks; `meleeHaste`, aura 319, the melee swings and the pet; `rangedHaste`, aura 140, the shots
  and the hawks. The hawks inherit the owner's ranged speed multiplier alone (wowsims `sim/core/pet.go:415-431`): the
  quiver, Quick Shots, Rapid Fire's ranged part, the ranged-only speed and an item's aura 342. Each item's aura is a
  factor of its own; two sums are the exceptions: the worn items' haste rating sums first (as ratings do) and the worn set
  bonuses' attack speed comes as the one sum `Score.SetBonuses` gives (two Tier-1 two-piece bonuses: 2% where two factors
  give 2.01%). Each input's weight is the value of one more 1% source of it at the worn point (`Model.Weights`: (1 + x/100)
  times the derivative by the input's percent), at which an item's, a set bonus's and a Use buff's attack speed is
  priced; a point of the summed haste rating or set bonus attack speed is so priced (1 + R/100) or (1 + S/100) times its
  model value. The pet's swing time is read without the owner's speeds it inherits (the owner's melee speed multiplier
  less the worn haste rating, divided out at its heartbeat); the model puts `haste`, `attackSpeed`, `meleeHaste` and Rapid
  Fire's average back on the pet's damage and on Frenzy's swing count. A worn item's Use buff of melee and ranged speed
  (Kiss of the Spider) counts at the melee-only and ranged-only weights over its uptime. HGA does not model the Troll
  racial Berserking. `/hga weights` prints the five inputs, their weights, the dual-wield hit weight and says when the
  stats are the last reading, with its reason: a Use buff up or unknown, or the game hiding the stats or the state of
  Aspect of the Hawk (of Trueshot Aura too with its talent, the only case the reading takes that aura's state) or of a
  stat multiplier aura.
- Live stats and Use buffs: the character sheet values (RAP, attack power, hit, crit, ranged haste, health, mana, armor) hold an item's "Use:" buff while it is up, and the score prices that buff over its uptime, so a reading counts only while every Use buff of `Game/UseData.lua` is known to be off, worn or not: an item used and then taken off may leave its buff on, and in the bags it is a candidate whose buff is priced (the buff is the used spell's own aura on the player, Forever DB2 SpellEffect; queried with the secret-safe aura check, `C_Secrets.ShouldSpellAuraBeSecret`; whether the buff stays after the item comes off is not settled by the sources at hand: no SpellEquippedItems row, while wowsims' simulator ends it on an item swap with no in-game log behind it). With one up or unknown, the last reading taken with none up is used, as for secret stats and an unknown Aspect of the Hawk / Trueshot Aura state. The reading also holds the live sheet's stat multipliers (`Game/StatMultData.lua`, generated by `tools/gen_stat_mult_data.py` from the DB2's auras 137 and 80, A_MOD_TOTAL_STAT_PERCENTAGE and A_MOD_PERCENT_STAT, as wowsims reads both: each aura up multiplies an item's Agility, Strength, Intellect and Spirit, Lightning Reflexes read as its rank; O8 = (a), RMD 4.8, 21, 24.3); a multiplier aura whose state the client keeps secret hides the reading the same way. Any such last reading serves only the gear and the applied talents it was taken with: per equipment slot and bag slot (a quiver's haste is in the reading), the item id, enchant id and suffix id the item link carries; the talent ranks and branch points as applied (talents re-applied out of combat change the sheet). With no such reading there is no evaluation: "hidden" while the stats are secret, else a reason saying a Use buff is up. While the evaluation waits on a Use buff — refused for that reason, or built from the last reading because of one — the Use buffs' aura state is checked every second (a timer; the same secret-safe check the reading takes, `Player.UseBuffState`); the evaluation stays as built while a Use buff is up or unknown, and the stats are re-read when it falls (every Use buff known to be off). The wait ends with the first evaluation that ends otherwise: the live reading used, the stats hidden, another reason.
- Swap banner: when putting on bag items gains damage (`Score.Plan`), a banner like Zygor's "new upgrade available" opens a list of what replaces what, each row with an «Надеть» (equip) button. A click equips that row as Blizzard's EquipmentManager does (`Swap.EquipRow`): pick the item up, check that it fits the slot (`C_PaperDollInfo.CursorCanGoInSlot`) and the slot is not locked, `EquipCursorItem`; in combat, with an item already on the cursor or when the bag item moved it says so in chat and equips nothing. A plan that takes an item off shows its row as «снять в сумку». The banner is always on out of combat (no setting, RMD 14.5) and hides in combat; its × hides it until the plan changes; `/hga swap` shows the list, also after the ×.
- `/hga dump` writes parsed items, raw tooltip lines and player stats (whether they are the last reading included) to SavedVariables so the parser can be checked against the real client; in raid mode with the worn set's fight (its segments, windows, mana regimes and means).
- Crit suppression and the attack tables (RMD 4.1-4.4): the level difference of every table capped at +3; a physical crit roll loses 1% a level of the difference, and at +3 the aura crit up to 1.8% more (wowsims, from EV-0090, Classic Era logs: no Forever log against a +3 target exists); the hunter's, the pet's and the hawks' white swings each one roll, the crit capped by the room left (wowsims OutcomeExpectedMeleeWhite); the pet and the hawks take the owner's physical and ranged-only gear hit.
- The raid fight's mana (RMD 7, 20): the casts' DB2 costs, the regen while casting (Bestial Discipline, Rapid Recuperation while Serpent Sting is up, Resourcefulness from the fight's ability crits), Judgement of Wisdom's 29.5 mana a landed hunter hit, the rich regime while the mana holds and the starved one after (cheapest ready first, the casts that drop), the consumables always on; `/hga weights` names when the mana runs out and what the fight buys after.

## Non-goals

- No equipping without the user's click: an item is put on only by the swap list's «Надеть» button, one row per click.

## Layout

- `Core/` — pure Lua 5.1, no WoW API: `Model.lua` (weights, the levelling pass and the raid fight), `Parse.lua` (tooltip lines → stats), `Score.lua` (set scoring, slot choice, MH/OH/2H pairing, procs).
- `Game/` — WoW glue: templates from `_G`, player state, item cache, tooltip, bags, quest, commands; generated DB2 data: `SetData.lua` (`tools/gen_set_data.py`), `UseData.lua` (`tools/gen_use_data.py`), `ProcData.lua` (`tools/gen_proc_data.py`), `StatMultData.lua` (`tools/gen_stat_mult_data.py`); each generator's docstring names its DB2 tables and its command line.
- `tests/` — run with `lua tests/run.lua` (Lua 5.4 with 5.1 shims); the set-bonus generator's rules that no bonus of the DB2 build exercises: `python -I tests/test_gen_set_data.py`; the proc generator's rules (`tools/gen_proc_data.py`, `Game/ProcData.lua`): `python -I tests/test_gen_proc_data.py`; the Use buff generator's pricing rule (`tools/gen_use_data.py` DAMAGE_KEYS): `python -I tests/test_gen_use_data.py`; the stat multiplier generator's rules (`tools/gen_stat_mult_data.py`): `python -I tests/test_gen_stat_mult_data.py`.
