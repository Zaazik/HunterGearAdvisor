## 4. The hawk fit — conflict resolved, and an arithmetic error found
- wowsims commit 77a901ae (Neteyes, 2026-11-11): "[Hunter] Hawk swings after the November 10 hotfix ... 0.0417 a point of the hawk's attack power, where logs before the hotfix read 0.0223 a point: 60 attack power a point of dps in place of 112". => 60 AP/DPS is POST-hotfix. R1-summon-hawk's "post-11/10: melee dmg *~1.8" on top of /60 double-applies the hotfix (R1 open question 4 is answered).
- summon_hawk.go lines 49-61: the swing was "read with Unleashed Fury 5/5 and Focused Fire 2/2 ... so the swing here is before them"; `AttackPowerPerDPS: 60 * hawkLogDamageMultiplier` (= 60 x 1.15 x 1.02 = 70.4 raw). => The observed 60 AP per DPS ALREADY CONTAINS UF 5/5 and FF 2/2. R2-hawk-ap-weapon says "(before UF 1.15, FF 1.02 ...)" — wrong; R2-pet-hawk-marginal multiplies by Mhawk = 1.173 again — double count (+17%).
- Recomputed (u=0.9, Hr=1.15, L=0.9, c=0.15, hawk crit 0.5c+0.10):
  - R2's written formula: hawks/RAP melee 0.0342 + dive 0.0064 = 0.0406 (R2 printed 0.036 — its number does not match its own formula).
  - Corrected (no extra 1.173): 0.0292 + 0.0064 = 0.0356 (~0.036).
  - Weapon DPS: R2 formula 0.479 (printed 0.43); corrected 0.409.
  - Pet: Mpet = 1.25 x 1.15 x 1.10 x 1.02 x 1.075 = 1.734; dPet/dRAP = 0.1/14 x 1.734 x 0.9 = 0.0111 (R2 0.012 ok).
  - "One hawk ~1.9x the pet per RAP": 0.8/60 vs 0.1/14 = 1.87 is the raw coefficient ratio only; with the multipliers (pet 1.73 incl. happiness/cat/BW; hawk already incl. UF/FF, x1.15 quiver haste) one hawk ≈ 1.24x the pet.
- Ammo in hawk AP: log 1180 at 747 RAP with a 27.3 dps bow -> 747+7+14x27.3 = 1136, residual 44 AP = 3.1 dps. A level-48 hunter's ammo is 12.5-15 dps (Hi-Impact 12.5, Jagged/Accurate 13, Mithril Gyro 15.2), so the residual is NOT consistent with full ammo inclusion (R2 said "consistent with ammo"). Note: 352 at 345 RAP "no weapons" -> the +7 = 14 x 0.5 dps (possibly the unarmed default) — speculation.
- Hawk crit 0.5 x inheritedCrit, hit = inheritedHit (physical + ranged-only), expertise = owner's, armor 30%, health 5% creature health, ranged-speed inheritance, damage multiplier = owner's at summon — all in summon_hawk.go (log-fitted, forever_web).
